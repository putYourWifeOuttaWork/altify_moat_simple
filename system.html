The whole system · One picture

One record. One door. One compounding loop.

This is not a diagram drawn to explain the system. It is the system's actual geometry, and the picture fell out of it. Every element means something, which is why the simple version is the true one.

Two worlds

A human world, where people sell and challenge the AI. A machine world, where the system verifies the work and holds the record.

One door

One filter at the exact center. All work passes through it, and nothing reaches the record any other way.

One loop

One path through both worlds, crossing the door twice a lap and compounding value on every pass.

The loop · Explore it

Two worlds. One door between them.

Up is high impact, down is low. Right is high enjoyment, left is low. Read any corner that way. Selling is both, which is why people belong there. A proactive brief sits bottom right: the person enjoys getting it and has no impact on making it. People work in the blue lobe, the machine works in the coral one, and every piece of work crosses the door at the center. Watch the ball: it swells where effort pays and drains where it does not.

What each region means

One lap. One seller's day.

They sell (top right). The work drops through the filter (protected, no raw writes, and they are spared the admin). The system verifies it into tiny truths (bottom left). HART surfaces what matters to their phone (bottom right). The verified change snapshots to D360 as it crosses the filter going up. They reconcile (top left: read the flag, challenge it, add what only they know, get auto-drafted next steps). They carry that enriched plan back into selling. Every lap the record gets deeper and the guidance gets better.

The door, HART, and reconciliation describe what Altify is building. Each is tagged below, and none of this is a shipped feature today.

Top right · Human work

Selling

The one thing only humans do. Highest impact, highest enjoyment.

This is where humans belong: in the room, on the phone, at dinner. The system's whole job is to protect this quadrant, to let a person put maximum effort here, because it is the only effort that actually wins deals. Nothing to log, nothing to update. They just sell. The work they do here is captured on the way out, not typed in.

The center · The filterDesigned, not yet shipped

One door into the record

One door into the record. No raw writes, ever.

Every piece of work crosses this one point, and it is the only way in. No surface, no agent, no copilot writes to the record directly. They propose in natural language, and this door decides what becomes true. That is how the record stays trustworthy no matter what is connected to it.

The door is lockedAn architectural fact, true by design. The only path into the record is through this filter.
Precision is a target we are climbingTarget 99.9%. Measured 99.73% on the first graded dataset. A goal we are climbing, not a property of the lock.
Bottom left · AI work

The engine

The admin and analysis humans shouldn't do. Done, and verified.

This is where the proprietary system turns raw sales activity into small verified facts, one at a time, each tied to the exact words that prove it, checked and voted before it is kept. Humans are bad at this and hate it; the system does it completely, so they never have to. This is the layer that makes everything above it trustworthy.

Bottom right · HARTForward looking

Human at the right time

The right thing, surfaced to the right person, before they ask.

Because the record is verified, the system can push what matters, unprompted, wherever the person works. Low effort for the human, high value: they are barely doing anything, which is exactly why they will actually use it. A brief when something matters, or a daily digest, by persona. This is HART, human at the right time. The purest expression of high enjoyment at near zero effort.

Top left · ReconciliationForward looking · how it will work

Challenge and enrich

Where humans challenge the AI and add what only they know.

This is the one quadrant that is a little bit work, and it is the highest leverage work in the loop. The human reads the guidance, challenges it, or adds the missing piece that would otherwise be lost forever, a puzzle piece other systems cannot even ask for, because they do not build around what a losing deal is shaped like. Then they carry that enriched plan straight back into selling.

Why it wins
Designed, not yet shipped
Negative ROI is structurally impossible.

Their verification is optional. Ours is a mandatory door. Because nothing enters the record unverified, no input can drag the return below zero.

Stated precisely: others do verify, but optionally, or with gaps. Here there is one door and no way around it.

This is a claim about the sign of the return, not its size. Human effort is spent only where it earns: fully in selling, a little in reconciliation, almost none in the admin the machine does.

This describes the design, not a shipped feature. Today production is read only, and the build lives in a developer sandbox.
